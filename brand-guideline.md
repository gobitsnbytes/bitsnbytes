# bits&bytes™ Brand Guidelines

## Logo

![logo](https://gobitsnbytes.org/logo)

The brand mark is a geometric cube rendered in a bold, isometric style with stylized B letterforms on its face and a four-pointed star on top. It works across light, dark, and solid-color backgrounds.

`public/logo.svg` is the white logo. Use it on dark, burgundy, orange, and high-contrast campaign surfaces. Use monochrome/dark variants only when a light surface requires them.

## Name Standards

**bits&bytes™** is the public brand name. Use this in all community-facing content, events, social posts, decks, merch, and design work. Always lowercase. The ampersand is part of the name.

**bitsnbytes** is the approved equivalent only where `&` is unavailable or awkward: domains, handles, repositories, package names, and file-system paths.

**GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION** is the registered legal entity name. Use it only in legal documents, contracts, invoices, regulatory filings, banking, and official correspondence.

Never write: "bits & bytes™", "Bits & Bytes", "bits and bytes", "B&B", "gobitsnbytes", or other public-facing variations.

## Visual Elements

The brand uses textural and graphic shapes to add energy without clutter:

- Gradient blobs: soft orange-to-burgundy radial warmth.
- Dither and halftone dots: retro print texture for loading states, overlays, and campaign surfaces.
- Burst/starburst shapes: bold burgundy attention marks.
- Star shapes: irregular black/burgundy editorial accents.

These elements should feel spontaneous, not chaotic. Pair them with generous white space and structured typography.

## Creative Standard

Every creative asset should feel like bits&bytes™: ambitious, student-led, technical, direct, and a little unruly in the right places. Use burgundy as the serious core, orange as controlled heat, and texture as motion. Avoid generic SaaS polish, over-glassy cards, and decorations that fight legibility.
