# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- Teens evaluating whether to join or participate
- Sponsors exploring partnerships
- Educators and community members learning about bits&bytes™

## Product Purpose

Create a memorable, high-impact public presence that makes visitors say "wow," drives sponsor interest, and supports a page-by-page redesign of the 4 public pages and 2 routes.

## Brand Personality

Innovate. Collaborate. Hack.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS or AI template layouts
- Hero-metric cliches and safe "modern" minimalism
- Overused gradients, glassy cards, and interchangeable bento grids

## Design Principles

- Lead with tangible outcomes over claims
- Make ambition feel credible, not flashy
- Build momentum through rhythm and contrast
- Show the community as builders, not spectators

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No explicit requirements provided.
