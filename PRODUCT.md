# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- Teens evaluating whether to join or participate
- Sponsors exploring partnerships
- Educators and community members learning about bits&bytes™

## Product Purpose

Create a memorable, high-impact public presence for bits&bytes™ that makes ambitious teenagers want to build, gives sponsors confidence, and keeps the organization’s public context accurate for humans and the on-page AI assistant.

## Brand Personality

Direct, student-led, production-minded, energetic, and just unruly enough to feel alive.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS or AI template layouts
- Hero-metric cliches and safe "modern" minimalism
- Overused gradients, glassy cards, and interchangeable bento grids

## Design Principles

- Lead with tangible outcomes over claims
- Make ambition feel credible, not flashy
- Build momentum through rhythm and contrast
- Show the community as builders, not spectators
- Use burgundy as the composed core, orange as controlled heat, and dither/halftone texture as subtle motion
- Keep navigation, hero spacing, and media loading calm enough that the craft disappears

## Current Content Priorities

- Hack4Good v0 is an archived event.
- The homepage secondary CTA is "Watch movie" and opens `public/movie/bnb-movie.mp4` in an in-page frame.
- `public/llms.txt` and `AGENTS.md` are the canonical RAG sources for public organizational context.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support reduced motion, keyboard-operable dialogs/menus, readable logo contrast, and safe handling of minor-related content.
